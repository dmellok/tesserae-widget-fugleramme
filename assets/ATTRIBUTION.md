## BirdNET scientific-name aliases

`birdnet_aliases.json` is an unmodified copy of the compiled
[`build/aliases.json`](https://github.com/tphakala/openfauna/blob/main/build/aliases.json)
from [OpenFauna](https://github.com/tphakala/openfauna) by
[tphakala](https://github.com/tphakala). It maps legacy BirdNET v2.4 scientific
names to their canonical taxa, so a plate filed under a bird's current name is
found under either name.

OpenFauna licenses this data under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The alias-map data here is therefore available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Bird sizes

`bird_sizes.csv` is body mass per species from AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022,
Ecology Letters, [doi:10.1111/ele.13898](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13898)),
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## BirdNET labels

`birdnet_labels_v2.4.txt` is the BirdNET v2.4 label list, by the Cornell Lab of
Ornithology and Chemnitz University of Technology.

## Classic plates

`artwork/classic/` holds the classic style's records only; see its
`ATTRIBUTION.md`. The plates themselves are fetched at runtime.
